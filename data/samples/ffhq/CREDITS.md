# Sample FFHQ images

Five test images from [FFHQ](https://github.com/NVlabs/ffhq-dataset) (Flickr-Faces-HQ, NVIDIA). They are FFHQ's
aligned and cropped 1024x1024 images (`images1024x1024/66000/`), resized to 256x256 by `scripts/prepare_natimg.py`.
All five photos were published on Flickr under the
[Public Domain Mark 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/) (from FFHQ's `ffhq-dataset-v2.json`):
